# Prismate Raw: one-knob air & presence enhancer

![Prismate Raw free one-knob air & presence enhancer UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/PrismateRaw/main/prismateraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob for instant, level-matched air, presence and clarity.**

Prismate Raw is the free, one-knob version of Prismate. Turn the **AIR** knob and it opens up your track, adding high-end air, a touch of presence and a low-mid clean-up in a single move, level-matched so it gets brighter and clearer, never just louder.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/PrismateRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`PrismateRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Prismate Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`PrismateRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Prismate Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **AIR** knob (0–100%). As you turn it up, Prismate Raw front-loads a single enhance move, a high-shelf air lift, a touch of presence, and a gentle low-mid clean-up, driven through a 2x oversampled ("Natural Phase") signal path. Built-in auto-gain holds the loudness so the knob adds shine and openness without turning the level up. A fixed DC blocker and an output soft-clipper keep the output clean and safe. Works on vocals, synths, drums, and the full mix.
